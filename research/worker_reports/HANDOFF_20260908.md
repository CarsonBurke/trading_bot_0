## The NextLat-informed line (arXiv:2511.05963v2) — full ledger

NextLat itself stays **REJECTED as a production mechanism**, twice, on two different grounds. First reading:
its update `h_{t+1} = h_t + MLP(LN([e(x_{t+1}), h_t]))` against stop-grad `h_{t+1}` predicts how to update
state *after seeing* the next observation — a posterior update, not a forecast; its own large-LM test
worsened held-out perplexity. Second reading, sharper and from our own numbers: oracle per-horizon
rescaling takes h=192 from 1.0212 to **0.99695**, so perfect amplitude buys 2.4% of long-end MSE and, by
construction, **exactly zero IC**. Any long-horizon gain must come from *information reaching the
forecast*, which NextLat's auxiliaries do not add. It was used as a SOURCE, not a prescription.

What came out of it, five items:

1. **Future-aware teacher — REJECTED WITH A PROOF, not an opinion.** A latent that has seen the realized
   window carries `y_{t,h}` itself in one coordinate and all seven `DECISION_HORIZONS` in seven, so IC is
   exactly **1.000 at d=512, d=32 and d=7 alike**: a width bottleneck restricts RANK, not information, and
   nothing in the stack provides a cheap channel-capacity constraint. Replaced by a **target-only**
   variance-ratio ceiling — no model fitted, zero training-step cost — and that substitution produced the
   session's best science: the plateau identity `ceil_h = c1`, the four-decimal ρ₁ agreement, and the
   measurement that `V_h/V_1` is state-dependent (64% swing).
2. **Orthonormal target rotation (Design A) — CUT.** Diagonal penalty: cumulative under a random walk
   **2.138** nats/bar, under AR(1) ρ₁=−.325 **1.867**, increments **0.0555** — the increment map consumes
   **97%** of the motivation. What remains wants a **3-tap causal prewhitener** (`M = L_Γ⁻¹A⁻¹`, `det M = 1`,
   100.0000% of 1.9065 nats/bar at **+0.30 ms**) not a dense DCT (+19.7 ms, 36,864 basis entries, recovers
   less). Code kept behind `--target-basis cumulative|haar|dct` — `cumulative` bit-exact identity — for the
   world where increments are rejected. Also doubly undecidable today: NLL incomparable across the basis
   boundary (2.1-nat free lunch), and its IC threshold was 0.3 SE.
3. **Frozen-trunk World A/B probe — BUILT, GREEN, UNMEASURED.** `probe-timexer-segment`, 8/8 tests,
   closed-form ridge + rank-8/32 principal probes on the post-final-RMSNorm latent at `origins-1`, i.e. a
   strict subset of the head's own input. Verdict rule is enforced in code, not prose. **No GPU run exists**
   — job 5458 was refused by its own leakage guard (which caught the real partition-granularity defect), and
   the gate is now **asymmetric**: the probe's fit block (2023-08-18→2024-08-15) is strictly more recent
   than anything the head trained on (targets end 2023-08-17), so World A needs `+0.010 + measured recency
   discount` and a failure is a *stronger* World B. The discount measurement is designed and unfunded:
   refit the same probe on an equal-size OLDER block, score on the same block, difference IS the advantage.
4. **Dyadic latent propagator — COSTED AND GATED, NOT BUILT.** Grid corrected: latents live on the 16-bar
   patch-token grid, so a grid dyadic in BARS is structurally blind below k=16 where the defect is worst
   (fitted gain 4.28 at h=1). Realizable grid is dyadic in TOKENS, k ∈ {1,2,4,8,12} = {16,32,64,128,192}.
   Costs: **+526,848 params (+1.94%), +1.49 TFLOP/step (+8.8%), ~+13.5 GB/step (+9.4%), +16-22 ms → 184-190
   ms**, peak ~19.6 of 25.9 GiB, and a latent arm must then pre-register **2,300 steps, not 2,500**.
   Strictly gated on World A from item 3. Do not build it before that number exists.
5. **The one NextLat idea that SURVIVED into a landed design.** "Eight pathways instead of 192
   near-duplicates" is now the **aggregation-supervision term** for `a(state)`: cumulative-space NLL on the
   CLOSE channel over a dyadic horizon set k = 1,2,4,…,192, means detached, `[rows, origins, 1, 8]` = 768k
   elements against 147M for the full block, ~**0.1% of the step**. It exists because `a(state)` gets no
   gradient from an increment-space objective and a frozen `A_h` is indefensible after the 64% swing.

Net: the paper's *mechanism* is rejected and its *framing* — supervise a small set of well-chosen pathways
rather than many near-duplicate ones — is what paid, both in the increment arm and in item 5.
